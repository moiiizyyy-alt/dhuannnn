Our place

Warm light, long tables, good smoke.

Dhuan means smoke, and that is where the flavour starts. Every kebab and chop is cooked over charcoal, and every naan is slapped onto the tandoor wall to order.

Come with family. The hall seats large groups, the outdoor area stays open late, and the kitchen runs until 1 am.
